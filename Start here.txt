YARN. Keep this open beside the other windows.
==============================================

You only do this once. Yarn stays on this Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

The Mac will refuse to open Setup the first time. Apple charges $99 a year
so that doesn't happen. I'm not paying that for one click. You will need
Privacy & Security, then Open Anyway. After that, it stays open.


1. Double-click Yarn Setup.command in this folder
   (the command next to this note)

   Expect “Not Opened”. That is step 2, not a mistake.
   If a welcome window does appear, skip to step 4.

   The window checks this Mac and says what it finds.
   If Yarn cannot run here, that window says why.


2. When the Mac says “Not Opened” and offers Done / Move to Bin

   Do not click Move to Bin.
   Click Done.

   Then:

   a. Open System Settings
      (Apple menu  →  System Settings)

   b. Click Privacy & Security in the left list

   c. Scroll to the Security section on the right
      (past Camera, Microphone, etc.)

   d. You should see a line about Yarn Setup.command, or Yarn Setup,
      being blocked.
      Click Open Anyway.

   e. If it asks again, click Open.

   If there is no Open Anyway button yet:
      Double-click Yarn Setup.command once more, click Done,
      then go back to Privacy & Security. The button
      appears after the Mac has tried to open it.


3. Optional, only if it still will not open

   Still in Privacy & Security, look for
   “Allow applications from” / “Allow apps downloaded from”.

   Choose App Store and identified developers
   (not “App Store only”).

   Then do step 2 again: Yarn Setup.command  →  Done  →  Open Anyway.


4. In the Yarn window

   Read the short welcome.
   Click Install Yarn.

   Leave the window open. The long bits are downloads.
   Your Mac may ask for:

   • Your password. That’s fine
   • Microphone access. Click Allow
   • A video call. If the Mac asks Yarn to record the screen, click Allow.
     It records the call audio, not the screen.
     If it does not ask, turn on Yarn under Privacy & Security.
     The row is called Screen Recording.
     On a newer Mac it is called Screen & System Audio Recording.
     Recording in the room does not ask.
   • To open Ollama. Click Open

   When it says you're all set, click Open Yarn.
   You are done with this note.
   The zip in Downloads can be deleted after that. Yarn now lives on this Mac.


5. After that

   Yarn is in the Dock, the row of icons along the bottom of the screen.
   Click it to start a recording, watch it process, name the voices,
   open the daily brief, or ask about your meetings.

   There is also a small Yarn icon in the menu bar (top of the screen)
   for the same controls.

   If the Dock icon or the menu-bar icon is missing, open Yarn from your
   Applications folder (the one inside your home folder). That puts both back.


If you get stuck, do not delete this folder.
Come back to this note and start from step 2.
