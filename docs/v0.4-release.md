# Welcome to Yarn

Your meetings. Your memories. Your computer.

Yarn records your meetings, figures out who said what, and turns conversations into searchable notes and a daily brief of what matters.

No subscriptions. No cloud AI processing. Your conversations stay on your Mac.

## What's new

- Settings has a Thread. Write a few lines about yourself, including your name. Ask Yarn uses that when it answers, and the grey example shows the shape of it.
- Stopping a recording opens one window: what the meeting was about, and who was in it. Names are separated by commas.
- In the library, Edit fixes a meeting name, and Delete removes the meeting and its transcript. Audio says Held while the recording is still on the Mac, and Purged after it expires.
- The menu bar percentage is the whole meeting, the same number as the Dock window.
- Setup installs both ffmpeg and ffprobe, and the Dock icon is a rounded tile.

## Getting started

1. Download Yarn.Setup.Apple.Silicon.zip from the Assets section.
2. Unzip it. You will see Start here.txt and Yarn Setup.command. Double-click the command.
3. Allow it in Privacy & Security. The Mac will stop you the first time.
4. Follow the installer.
5. Open Yarn and start recording.

Apple would like $99 a year so you don't have to click a button once. I'm not paying that. When the Mac says Yarn Setup was not opened, click Done, not Move to Bin. Then System Settings, Privacy & Security, and Open Anyway. Start here.txt in the zip walks through the same clicks.

If you already use Yarn, run Yarn Setup.command from this zip. That replaces the app. A headphone call may need Yarn turned on again under System Settings, Privacy & Security, Screen & System Audio Recording.

Yarn currently supports Apple Silicon Macs.
