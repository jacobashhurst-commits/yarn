Yarn

Your meetings. Your memories. Your computer.

Yarn records a meeting when you press start, writes down who said what, and keeps it on your Mac.

Download for Apple Silicon

One zip. Apple Silicon Macs only. No account. Nothing is uploaded.

After the download

The zip is the installer. The speech model and a few other pieces download next, onto this Mac, while a window stays open. That is the slow part. You only do it once.

  1. 1

    Open the zip

    It lands in Downloads. Double-click it. You should see Yarn Setup and a note called Start here.

  2. 2

    Double-click Yarn Setup

    Not the bin folder. The Yarn Setup file sitting next to the note.

  3. 3

    If the Mac says it was not opened

    It will offer Done and Move to Bin.

    Click Done. Do not click Move to Bin.

    Then open System Settings from the Apple menu. Click Privacy & Security. Scroll to the Security section, past Camera and Microphone. When you see Yarn Setup blocked, click Open Anyway. If it asks again, click Open.

    If Open Anyway is not there yet, double-click Yarn Setup once more, click Done, and look again. The button appears after the Mac has tried to open it.

  4. 4

    Click Get started, and leave the window open

    The long parts are downloads. Your Mac may ask for your password, the microphone, SwiftBar, and Ollama. Those are fine. Click Allow or Open.

    When it says Yarn is ready, it is in the Dock. Click that icon to record. There is also a small Yarn icon in the menu bar.

Everything Yarn keeps stays on this Mac. The source is on GitHub if you want to read it. This page is the download.
