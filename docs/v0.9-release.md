# Welcome to Yarn

Your meetings. Your memories. Your computer.

Yarn records your meetings, figures out who said what, and turns conversations into searchable notes and a daily brief of what matters.

No subscriptions. No cloud AI processing. Your conversations stay on your Mac.

## What's new

- The first time you press Headphones, Yarn asks what to call you. That name is you on the recording.
- After you name the other voices, the saved list includes you as well, the same way a room recording lists each person.
- If Yarn is not in the Screen & System Audio Recording list, click + and choose Yarn from the Applications folder in your home folder.

## Getting started

1. Download Yarn.Setup.Apple.Silicon.zip from the Assets section on this page.
2. Unzip it and double-click Yarn Setup.
3. Click Install Yarn and leave the window open.
4. Open Yarn and start recording.

If you already use Yarn, double-click Yarn Setup from this zip. That replaces the app. The first headphone call on this signed Yarn may need Yarn added once: click + under System Settings, Privacy & Security, Screen & System Audio Recording, and choose Yarn from the Applications folder in your home folder. On an older Mac that row is called Screen Recording.

Yarn currently supports Apple Silicon Macs.
